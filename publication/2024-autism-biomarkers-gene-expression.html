    Unraveling the Genetic Tapestry: Exploring Biomarkers for Autism Spectrum Disorder Detection through Gene Expression Analysis

    Published in Jagannath University — Undergraduate Research, 2024

    Overview

    This research investigates biomarkers for Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) detection through gene expression analysis, applying machine-learning techniques to identify children with ASD as early as possible.

    • Institution: Jagannath University, Dhaka, Bangladesh
    • Duration: February 2024 – August 2024
    • Objective: Accurately detect autism in young children using machine learning on gene-expression data.

    Key Results

    • Achieved 97% classification accuracy.
    • 1% improvement over the previous state-of-the-art model.
    • Contributed to data analysis, feature selection, model training, and evaluation.

    Techniques

    • Machine Learning · Predictive Modeling
    • Gene Expression Data Processing
    • Feature Selection & Biomarker Analysis
    • Model Evaluation & Comparative Benchmarking
