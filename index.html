About Me

I'm a Backend Engineer at Shikho Technologies Ltd., where I build the distributed backend infrastructure that powers one of Bangladesh's largest edtech platforms. My primary tools are Golang, MongoDB, and Redis.

I care deeply about clean architecture, performance engineering, and writing systems that are as maintainable as they are fast. I've built real-time analytics pipelines, AI-assisted content tooling, and high-scale quiz APIs that serve concurrent users with sub-100ms latency.

Outside of work, I stay sharp through competitive programming — with over 1000+ problems solved on Codeforces, LeetCode, and other platforms. I've represented my university at ICPC Asia Regional and ranked 6th in Bangladesh at IEEEXtreme 17.0.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Jagannath University, Dhaka, where I also published undergraduate research on ML-based ASD detection achieving 97% accuracy.
